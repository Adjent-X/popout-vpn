The installer **downloads and executes** [angristan/openvpn-install](https://github.com/angristan/openvpn-install) by [Stanislas Lange](https://github.com/angristan) when OpenVPN is not already present. That script is copyright its authors and licensed under **GPL-3.0**. We do not vendor, relicense, or claim it. If you decline that installer, you must supply a working OpenVPN server under `/etc/openvpn/server/` yourself.
