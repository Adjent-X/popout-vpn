# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Popout VPN a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

- Be respectful. Assume good intent, then be precise.
- No harassment, doxxing, or sexualized language.
- No “free pentest” against other operators’ VPN boxes.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers via GitHub or the security contact in SECURITY.md. Maintainers may warn, limit, or ban contributors.

This is a short form of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
